A chat is a line. Your questions aren’t.

Most topics are layered: one big question made of several parts, each with its own follow-ups. A chat window only lets you go down one part at a time, and the rest scrolls away.

In a normal chat

How do I start a bakery?
Four parts: licensing, equipment, suppliers, pricing…
Tell me more about licensing.
Licensing needs a permit, a health check…
…and the equipment and pricing questions you meant to ask are now far up the page.

You lose the thread, and you never see the whole picture.

On DSH Canvas

A tree with the main question on the left and four branches: licensing, equipment, suppliers and pricing. Licensing has two deeper follow-ups.

Every part has its own branch. Dashed ones are still waiting for you. Click one and ask.

  • See the layersThe main question sits at the root. Each part branches out, and each follow-up goes one level deeper.
  • Return to the exact contextClick the reply where you left off and ask the question you skipped. The earlier answers stay as they were.
  • Understand the whole topicWhen you’re done, the tree is a map of everything you explored, instead of a long scroll to re-read.

A conversation that can go more than one way

A chat is a single line. Real thinking isn’t. DSH Canvas keeps every path you try.

Ask

Start a session in any folder on your Mac. Your question and the reply appear as cards on the canvas.

Branch

Pick any earlier reply and ask again from there. The new question grows a new branch; the old one stays exactly as it was.

Find it again

Tag cards with labels, then search across every canvas, text and labels together, and jump straight to the card.

Labels and search

Works in your folders

Your model, your key

Choose a provider, paste a key, pick a model and a thinking effort. Switch whenever you like.

Local first

Canvases, settings, and your key live on your computer. There is no account and no cloud sync.

Bring the model you already use

Pick a provider in Settings and paste your API key. Nothing else to configure.

  • DeepSeek
  • OpenRouter
  • OpenAI
  • Anthropic
  • Google Gemini
  • Any OpenAI-compatible endpoint

For a custom endpoint, enter its base URL and key. The model list is fetched automatically, and you can add model names by hand if the endpoint doesn’t publish one.

Your conversations stay yours

DSH Canvas runs entirely on your Mac. The only network traffic is between the app and the model provider you choose.

  • Stored on your computer. Canvases and settings are plain files in your user library. Delete the folder and they’re gone.
  • Key never leaves for us. Your API key is sent only to the provider you picked. We run no server that sees it.
  • Closed to other programs. The app’s local service listens only on your machine, on a random port, and requires a secret that only its own window holds.

Download for macOS

  1. Open the downloaded .dmg and drag DSH Canvas into Applications.
  2. Open it from Applications. Because the app is not yet signed with an Apple Developer ID, macOS may stop the first launch.
  3. If it does, open System Settings → Privacy & Security and choose “Open Anyway”. If macOS says the app is damaged, run this in Terminal and open it again:
xattr -cr "/Applications/DSH Canvas.app"

A signed and notarized build, with automatic updates, is on the way.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. DSH Canvas is free to download and use. You only pay your model provider for the requests you make with your own key.

Is there a Windows or Intel Mac version?

Not yet. The first release is for Apple silicon Macs. Other platforms will follow.

Do I need an account?

No. You need an API key from the model provider you want to use, and nothing else.

Where is my data stored?

In ~/Library/Application Support/DSH Canvas. Removing that folder removes your canvases and settings.

What is DSH?

DSH is the open-source agent engine that DSH Canvas runs locally to talk to models and work with your files. DSH Canvas is the canvas interface built around it.
