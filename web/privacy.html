Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Thinkfork runs on your Mac. We operate no servers for it, and we do not collect, receive or store your conversations, files, API keys or usage data.

What stays on your computer

  • Your canvases, settings, attachments and recovery snapshots are stored locally. The About page shows the app data directory. Mac App Store builds use the app’s sandbox container.
  • Model API keys and Thinkfork-managed MCP credentials are stored in local encrypted records protected by macOS Keychain. If you sign in to a remote MCP service through your browser (OAuth), the access and refresh tokens are stored in the same protected records and are deleted when you sign out or remove the connection.
  • Session records created by the assistant engine are also stored locally.

To remove app-managed local data, locate the data directory shown in About, then quit Thinkfork before removing it. Independently saved workspace files, backups and data retained by external providers are managed separately.

What is sent, and to whom

  • Your chosen model provider. Your messages, the parts of your files and command results the assistant needs, and your API key are sent directly from your Mac to the provider you configure (for example DeepSeek, OpenRouter, OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, or an endpoint you enter). We are not a party to that exchange. The provider’s own privacy policy and terms apply to it.
  • Remote MCP services you enable. The service receives connection requests and the tool arguments for calls made on your behalf. Its own privacy policy and retention rules apply. If you choose browser sign-in (OAuth), Thinkfork opens that service’s own sign-in page in your default browser and receives the result on a temporary local address on your Mac. Thinkfork never sees your password, and the tokens are sent only to that service and the authorization server it names.
  • Updates. The Mac App Store edition is updated through the App Store and makes no update check of its own. Earlier builds that were downloaded from this website could request a small file (release.json) from this website to see whether a newer version exists; that request contains no identifier, canvas or conversation.
  • Model lists. When you ask the app to fetch a provider’s model list, it contacts that provider.
  • Things you ask the assistant to do. If you ask it to browse the web, run commands, or connect to a service, those actions use the network as you directed.

Telemetry

The assistant engine that Thinkfork runs includes optional telemetry. Thinkfork turns it off. The app has no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting of its own.

This website

The site is a static page hosted on GitHub Pages, which receives your IP address under GitHub’s privacy statement. It sets no cookies and has no analytics. Your browser remembers your language choice locally. Links to the Mac App Store open Apple’s site, which has its own privacy policy.

Children

Thinkfork is not directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: use the support page.

隐私政策

最近更新:2026 年 11 月 23 日

Thinkfork 运行在你的 Mac 上。我们没有为它运行任何服务器,也不会收集、接收或保存你的对话、文件、API key 和使用数据。

保存在你电脑上的内容

  • 画布、设置、附件和恢复快照保存在本机。“关于”页面会显示数据目录，Mac App Store 版本使用应用的沙盒容器。
  • 模型 API Key 与 Thinkfork 管理的 MCP 凭据保存在受 macOS 钥匙串保护的本机加密记录中。如果你通过浏览器（OAuth）登录远程 MCP 服务，访问令牌和刷新令牌也保存在同样受保护的记录中，并在你退出登录或移除连接时删除。
  • 助手引擎生成的会话记录也保存在本机。

如需移除应用管理的本机数据，请先在“关于”页面找到数据目录，再退出 Thinkfork 后移除。单独保存的工作区文件、备份和远程服务保留的数据分别管理。

会发送什么、发送给谁

  • 你选择的模型提供方。你的消息、助手需要用到的文件片段和命令结果,以及你的 API key,会直接从你的 Mac 发送给你配置的提供方(例如 DeepSeek、OpenRouter、OpenAI、Anthropic、Google,或你填写的接口)。我们不参与这一过程,适用的是该提供方自己的隐私政策和条款。
  • 你启用的远程 MCP 服务。服务会收到连接请求，以及代表你调用工具时传入的参数。服务自己的隐私政策与保留规则适用。如果你选择浏览器登录（OAuth），Thinkfork 会在默认浏览器中打开该服务自己的登录页面，并通过 Mac 本机的临时地址接收结果。Thinkfork 不会看到你的密码，令牌只会发送给该服务及其指定的授权服务器。
  • 更新。Mac App Store 版本通过 App Store 更新，自身不做更新检查。此前从本网站下载的旧版本可向本网站请求一个小文件（release.json）来查看是否有新版本；该请求不包含任何标识、画布或对话内容。
  • 模型列表。当你让应用获取某个提供方的模型列表时,它会连接该提供方。
  • 你要求助手做的事。如果你让它浏览网页、运行命令或连接某个服务,这些操作会按你的指示使用网络。

遥测

Thinkfork 所运行的助手引擎自带可选的遥测功能,Thinkfork 已将其关闭。应用本身没有统计分析、广告、跟踪或崩溃上报。

本网站

本站是托管在 GitHub Pages 上的静态页面，GitHub 会按其隐私声明获取你的 IP 地址。本站不设置 Cookie，也没有统计分析。浏览器会在本地记住你选择的语言。指向 Mac App Store 的链接会打开 Apple 的网站，适用其自己的隐私政策。

未成年人

Thinkfork 不面向 13 岁以下的儿童。

变更与联系

本政策如有变更,新版本会连同新的日期发布在此页。如有疑问，请使用支持页面。
